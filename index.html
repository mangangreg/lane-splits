m — 1.22 is the World Athletics standard
Distances use the World Athletics model: 84.39 m straights, a 36.50 m inner radius, and a measure line 30 cm out from the kerb in lane 1 and 20 cm out from the inner line in every other lane. Lane 1 therefore comes out at exactly 400.00 m and lane 8 at 453.03 m.
Pace is held constant, so the extra metres are extra work, not a faster effort. A 400 m assumption in lanes 3–4 would have you running roughly 4.6% faster than intended for the whole session.
